How to choose a product

Start with your use case. List the two or three features that genuinely affect how you will use the product. Set a realistic budget, compare alternatives and check current reviews, warranty and return terms before buying.

1. Define the job

A product that is perfect for one person can be unnecessary for another. Decide what you need it to do.

2. Compare the important differences

Ignore specs that do not affect your use. Prioritize comfort, reliability, compatibility, support, battery life, durability and other factors relevant to the category.

3. Check total value

Consider the purchase price, accessories, replacement costs, warranty and expected lifespan.

How to find value

The cheapest product is not always the best value. A slightly more expensive product may be a better fit if it solves your needs more reliably or lasts longer.
