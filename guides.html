Decide better.

Simple frameworks for choosing products without getting lost in endless specifications.

How to choose tech

Start with the job you need the product to do. Then identify the few specifications that materially affect that job. For headphones, that might mean comfort, sound profile, battery and microphone quality. For a laptop, it might mean performance, screen, battery and repairability.

Don't buy the spec sheet

A bigger number is not automatically a better experience. Compare real-world use, compatibility, warranty and reviews alongside specifications.

How to find value

Value is the relationship between what you pay and what you actually get. Consider the upfront price, accessories, maintenance, warranty, expected lifespan and return policy.

How to spot a good deal

Check the current price against recent prices when possible, then confirm seller reputation, warranty and return terms. A large discount badge does not by itself prove that something is a good deal.
